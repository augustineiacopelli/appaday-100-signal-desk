Analyst workbench

Point it at a spreadsheet. Get the first hour of analysis back.

Signal Desk reads a CSV the way an analyst does: it sniffs the delimiter, infers what every column actually holds, profiles each distribution, scores the data quality, correlates what is numeric, and proposes only the charts the shape of your data supports. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. The file is parsed in your browser.

Drop a CSV here
01

Type inference

Every column is tested against integer, decimal, currency, percent, date, boolean, category and identifier patterns before anything is measured.

02

Distribution profiling

Quartiles, spread, skew, and outliers by both the interquartile rule and z-score, with a histogram per numeric field.

03

Quality scoring

Missingness, duplicate rows, constant columns, mixed types and outlier load roll into one graded score with the reasons shown.

04

Correlation

Pearson across every numeric pair, drawn as a heatmap and ranked so the strongest relationships surface first.

05

Cohort retention

When an identifier repeats alongside a date and a value, the retention triangle, revenue curves and lifetime value build themselves.

06

AI briefing

Claude reads the computed profile, never the raw rows, and writes the executive summary. The statistics stay yours.

These charts were chosen by the shape of your data, and each one explains the rule that produced it. Tap any bar, point or segment to open the rows behind it, or build your own above.

Build your own

A pivot crosses two fields at once. Pick what goes down the side, what goes across the top, and what gets measured in the middle. Every cell, label and total is tappable.

Build a pivot

Compare two groups side by side. The app measures every other field in both groups and ranks them by how different they are, so you see what actually separates the two.

Compare segments

Drivers break a change into pieces. Pick a measure and a field to split it by, and the app shows exactly how much each group added to the change or took away from it. Every bar and every row is tappable.

Explain a change

A cohort is everyone who first appeared in the same period. Press Build cohorts, then read left to right to see how many came back. Tap any cell for the entities inside it.

Cohort Copilot

A forecast projects the pattern already in your history forward. Several standard methods are fitted, scored against periods held back from them, and the one that actually performed best is used. It cannot see anything that has not happened yet.
